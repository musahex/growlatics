## 1. What Growlatics is
International **Growth Operations + BPO + Technology Partner**. US / Pakistan-based. Markets: US, UK, Pakistan, plus other international. NOT cheap outsourcing, NOT generic agency, NOT Pakistan-only, NOT marketing-only. Must feel capable for serious, established businesses.
Ideal client: established and growth-stage businesses that need extra marketing, sales, customer-operations or technology capability without coordinating several disconnected vendors. Do not over-narrow industry. Commercial priority: **Sales & BPO**; brand sells the advantage of connecting functions.

## 2. Core problem / thesis
Companies run growth through disconnected providers (marketing, SEO, sales, support, tech). Leads, information and accountability leak between them. Growlatics connects them into one operating system.
Central message: **Growlatics builds and operates the systems behind growth.** Everything reinforces it.

## 3. Service universe (organise, don't dump in Hero)
- SALES & BPO: sales support, BPO, business-process outreach, cold outreach, appointment setting, inbound, outbound, telesales, lead qualification, sales operations.
- MARKETING: performance, digital, social, SEO, paid campaigns, lead gen, funnel optimisation, YouTube performance.
- CUSTOMER OPERATIONS: customer support/service ops, chat, call, retention workflows, CX operations.
- TECHNOLOGY: web, ecommerce, digital products, automation, full-stack, UI/UX, supporting digital systems.
Suggested grouping: **Acquire → Sell → Operate → Build** (or stronger). Sales/BPO slightly more prominent.

## 4. Primary CTA
